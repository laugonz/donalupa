Privacy Policy

Limited gameplay analytics. No accounts, ads or cross-app tracking.

No accounts, no tracking

Doña Lupa has no sign-up, login, ads. Your saved game progress remains on your device and in the device backups you control.

Gameplay analytics

To improve puzzle difficulty and find places where players get stuck, the app sends limited product-interaction summaries to our server: case identifier, app and content version, language, elapsed time, completion or suspension, progress counts, hints, reviews, resets and incorrect attempts. A fictional character identifier may be included after 90 seconds without progress. We do not send names, free text, exact board coordinates or the full move history.

Each play attempt has a random identifier. Starting with app 1.4, gameplay events can also include the existing random Superwall user ID, allowing us to count distinct app users and whether they play again on later days. This is a persistent pseudonymous identifier, not a name or email; a reinstall or reset can create another ID. We do not create a second installation ID or collect an advertising ID for gameplay analytics. Technical context includes distribution channel (App Store or TestFlight), device model, operating system version, device language and, when available, the App Store country. The store country is not your physical location. These gameplay records are retained for 90 days and are not used for advertising.

Purchases

Apple processes payments through the App Store; we do not receive your payment card details. We use Superwall to deliver purchase screens and measure app sessions, onboarding, purchase-screen interactions and purchase results. Superwall uses a persistent random user ID and technical device and app information. We do not send it a name or email. See Superwall’s privacy policy.

Content updates

The app periodically downloads new cases from our server. Like any web server, it keeps standard technical logs such as IP address and timestamp for security and operations. These logs are not linked to an identity and are routinely deleted.
