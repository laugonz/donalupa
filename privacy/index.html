Privacy Policy

Doña Lupa — last updated August 15, 2026

We do not collect personal data.

No accounts, no tracking

Doña Lupa has no sign-up, no login, no ads and no third-party analytics. Your game progress is stored only on your device (and in your device backups, which you control).

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The screen that offers the purchase is delivered by a remote configuration service; it operates on anonymous, device-level information and involves no personal data.

Content updates

The app periodically downloads new cases from our server. Like any web server, it keeps standard technical logs (IP address, timestamp) for security and operations; these are not linked to any identity and are routinely deleted.

Contact

Questions? Visit Support.

Política de privacidad

No recopilamos datos personales.

Doña Lupa no tiene registro, ni cuentas, ni anuncios, ni analíticas de terceros. Tu progreso se guarda solo en tu dispositivo (y en tus copias de seguridad, que controlas tú). Las compras las procesa Apple al completo: nunca vemos tus datos de pago. La pantalla de compra la sirve un servicio de configuración remota que opera con información anónima del dispositivo, sin datos personales. La app descarga casos nuevos de nuestro servidor, que guarda únicamente registros técnicos estándar (IP y fecha), no vinculados a ninguna identidad y borrados de forma rutinaria.
