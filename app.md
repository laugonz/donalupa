## Audience and positioning

Doña Lupa is for adults and older puzzle players who enjoy sudoku, logic-grid puzzles, cozy mysteries and dry humor. It is not a violent crime game. The differentiator is the combination of sudoku constraints, illustrated suspects and short comic mystery payoffs.

Core message:

> Murder sudoku, with suspects.

Spanish equivalent:

> Un sudoku con sospechosos.

### On-page placement

- Use the primary phrase naturally in the title, H1, opening answer, meta description and one descriptive image alt.
- Use supporting phrases only where they improve an H2, example, caption or explanation.
- Add one contextual link from each guide to its two related guides and one link back to the relevant product landing.
- Add a mid-article App Store CTA after the reader has learned something useful.
- Add two unique, visible FAQs to every guide and keep `FAQPage` JSON-LD exactly synchronized with those answers.
- Use `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage` and, only on procedural guides, `HowTo` structured data.
- Exception: the MURDOKU mark may appear naturally only on the independent comparison page. Do not apply the usual exact-match placement rule mechanically or repeat the mark to increase density.

## Content and claim guardrails

- Never claim unlimited free access: easy cases are free; the full collection is a one-time unlock.
- Never imply a subscription or advertise one: there is none.
- Do not invent case counts, ratings, awards, player counts or future release dates.
- Do not publish aggregate-rating schema until the listing has enough stable review volume to be representative.
- `FAQPage` structured data is allowed only when every question and answer is visibly present and matches the markup.
- Product screenshots must remain recognizably faithful to the current app and preserve their 630:1369 aspect ratio.
- New guides must answer a real puzzle-player question and link naturally to the app; avoid thin keyword pages.
- Do not describe the stories as bloody, scary or realistic crime; the live listing positions them as comic, cozy mysteries.

## Trademark guardrail

- MURDOKU is European Union word mark no. `019308162`, filed on 2026-01-26 and registered on 2026-07-29 for Studios Digivoid Inc. Its listed goods and services include logic puzzles, online games and downloadable/mobile logic games.

## Visual direction

Warm paper, dark brown ink, orange evidence marks, rounded editorial typography and real illustrated suspects. The site should feel like a playful case file, not a generic SaaS landing page. Guide images should extend this world with distinct editorial scenes, not imitate the app UI or add illegible generated text.
