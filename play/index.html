How this case works

Each piece, including the lunchbox, needs its own row and column. Solid furniture blocks squares. Room names appear in every square. Thick lines separate rooms; thin lines separate squares in the same room. The culprit is the only suspect sharing a room with the lunchbox.

Read the deduction, step by step
  1. The water cooler places the accountant at row 1, column 3.
  2. In the desks area, only the standing desk is available: marketing goes at row 2, column 4.
  3. The vending machine places the sales rep at row 3, column 2.
  4. Only row 4 and column 1 remain, so the lunchbox is at the coffee machine.
  5. The sales rep and the lunchbox are together in the break area. That identifies the culprit.
